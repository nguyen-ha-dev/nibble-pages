Nibble Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

The Short Version

Nibble does not collect anything about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing you save is sent to its developer.

Your Spots

The spots, visits, hearts and notes you save are kept on this iPhone.

If you are signed in to iCloud, Nibble also backs them up to your own private iCloud storage. Apple stores that backup for you. The developer cannot read it.

Sync With Partner

Partner sync is off until you send an invite. When you do, your Doubles list is shared with the one person you invite, through iCloud. They can see the name on your iCloud account, and you can see theirs.

Your Solo list is never shared. You can disconnect in Settings at any time, and you both keep a copy of the spots.

Location and Search

If you allow it, Nibble uses your location on this iPhone to show the spots near you. Nibble works without it.

Searching for a place uses Apple Maps: the text you type and the area being searched are sent to Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. Directions open in Apple Maps.

Backup Files

Export to Files writes a backup file to the place you choose. Nibble does not send it anywhere.

Deleting Your Data

Delete all spots in Settings removes your spots from this iPhone, and from your iCloud backup the next time Nibble syncs. Deleting the app removes everything it saved on this iPhone.

Changes and Contact
