Nibble Support

Nibble is a restaurant wishlist and visit journal for iPhone, made by Nguyen Ha.

Contact

Email newwenha@gmail.com with a question, a problem or an idea. Say which iPhone and iOS version you use, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common Questions

Where are my spots kept? On your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud storage if you are signed in to iCloud.

How do I move my spots to a new iPhone? Sign in to the same iCloud account and open Nibble. You can also use Export to Files in Settings on the old iPhone and Import from Files on the new one.

How do I share a list with my partner? Switch to Doubles in Settings, then tap Sync with partner and send the invite. Nibble must be installed on both iPhones.

How do I delete everything? Settings, then Delete all spots.
